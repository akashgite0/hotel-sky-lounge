Our Story

A tower lit up on Nila Road.

In April 2023, a tower on Nila Road lit up for the first time. Nanded had known good food for years — but Sky Lounge arrived with a different idea: a restaurant that feels like an occasion, every single evening. A room above the city's rush, where the lights come on as the sun goes down.

The house keeps two rooms under one roof — a calm family dining room, and a bar of its own — so every kind of evening has its place. Between them, one kitchen, faithful to fire: clay ovens, sealed handis, and marinades that rest a full day before they ever meet the coals.

What guests return for is simple: biryanis sealed on dum, kebabs from the tandoor, and the feeling of the tower at night, glowing over Nila Road.

“Unhurried food, warm light, and a table that is always glad to see you.” The Sky Lounge Way
