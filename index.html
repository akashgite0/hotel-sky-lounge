01 — The Restaurant

A table set for the family, a fire kept for the flavour.

On Nila Road in Nanded, Sky Lounge keeps two rooms under one roof — a calm family dining room, and a full bar of its own. Between them, one kitchen: clay ovens, slow handis, and biryanis guests return for.

02 — From the Fire

Slow food,
cooked fast & hot.

Marinades rest for a day. Handis seal and slow-cook for hours. Kebabs meet the clay oven only when the coals are right. Nothing here is hurried except the service.

Come at night, when the tower lights up and the whole building glows over Nila Road — that is when Sky Lounge feels most itself.
